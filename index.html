About Us

A School Where Every Child Belongs

Ireme Academy offers early childhood and primary education for children aged 1 to 11 years in Kinyinya Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali City.

Since opening on 15th April 2018, we have been committed to creating a safe, caring, and inclusive environment where children from all backgrounds can learn, grow, and thrive.

Through dedicated educators and a supportive, home-like setting, we aim to make education accessible and affordable while nurturing confident learners and future leaders.

🔭 Vision

Nurturing confident, responsible future leaders.

🎯 Mission

Providing a safe, caring, and affordable environment where every child learns, grows, and leads.

Admissions

Join Our School Family

Enrolling your child is simple and straightforward. Follow the steps below or fill in the form and we will respond within 2 business days.

How to Apply

  1. 1
    Submit an Enquiry
    Fill in the online form, visit our school office, or contact us on WhatsApp at +250 788 281 186.
  2. 2
    School Visit & Assessment
    We invite parents and the child for a friendly tour and an informal assessment suited to their age.
  3. 3
    Receive an Offer
    If a place is available, we send a formal offer of admission within 5 working days.
  4. 4
    Complete Registration
    Submit required documents and pay the registration fee to confirm your child's place.
  5. 5
    Welcome to Ireme Academy!
    Your child attends an orientation day to meet their teacher, classmates, and settle into their new school.
📎 Documents Required
  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Parent / guardian national ID or passport
  • Parent home and work addresses
  • Passport-size photo of the child
  • Most recent school report (if applicable)
